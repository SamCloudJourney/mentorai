**Why it worked — the 7 ingredients (this is the transferable DNA):**
1. **Cyclical commodity** — DRAM/NAND are priced commodities with violent boom/bust ASPs.
2. **Oligopoly supply** — just 3 players (Micron, SK Hynix, Samsung). Critically, this cycle they showed **supply discipline** — they did *not* recycle rising margins straight back into capex like every prior cycle. That handed **pricing power** back to producers.
3. **Brutal operating leverage** — memory is ~all fixed cost, so a price turn doesn't add a little profit, it *multiplies* it (22% → 81% gross margin; EPS +651%).
4. **A deep trough** — losses + a glut = maximum pessimism, minimum expectations.
5. **A NEW secular demand driver bolted on top** — **AI / High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM)**. HBM capacity is **sold out through CY2026** across all three players; Micron's HBM runs at ~$8B annualized and it just **overtook Samsung to ~21% share** ([Astute](https://www.astutegroup.com/news/general/sk-hynix-holds-62-of-hbm-micron-overtakes-samsung-2026-battle-pivots-to-hbm4/)). This is the magic ingredient: it converted a *normal cyclical bounce* into a *multi-year supercycle* and forced a **re-rating** — the market stopped pricing MU as a "dumb cyclical" and started pricing it as a "structural AI winner."
6. **Capital intensity / long lead-times** — new fabs take years + tens of billions, so supply can't respond fast → the shortage persists.
7. **Optically expensive at the trough, optically cheap at the top** — the tell most people get backwards. At the bottom the P/E is negative/insane (no earnings). After a +293% run it trades at **~5.7x forward earnings (FY26), ~10x on FY27** — *still* 80% below the sector, because earnings outran the price.

**The behavioral signature (the chart story):** Price **bottomed while the company was still losing billions** — it led reported earnings by ~4 quarters. The fattest gains came *before* the blowout profits printed, at the point of maximum "why would anyone own this commodity" disgust. You had to buy the loss and the glut.

**The one honest warning (so you respect the risk):** it's *still cyclical.* Goldman flags that **today's ~80% margins are a peak, not a floor** — synchronized capacity expansion eventually compresses prices again. The Micron trade is about catching the *trough-to-recognition* leg; you do not marry a commodity at peak margins.
